Admissions

Join SwiftSchool

Our admissions process is designed to be simple and welcoming.

1. Enquiry

Contact the school or visit us to learn about programmes, available places and admission requirements.

2. Application

Submit the required application information and supporting documents.

3. Assessment

Where applicable, students may participate in an assessment or interview.

Typical Requirements

  • Completed admission application
  • Previous school records where applicable
  • Birth certificate or identification document
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Parent or guardian information

Contact our admissions office for current admission dates, fees and class availability.
